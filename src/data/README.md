# Owner's data: read only

These files are the owner's knowledge base, rules and Vietnamese text. The app reads them; it never
changes them. Every cultural or historical sentence on screen comes from here, word for word.

- kb.json: GARMENTS (9 áo: structure, parts, timeline, sources…), COLORS, MODERN_PIECES, ACCESSORIES, SOURCES, PART_LABELS_VI
- doll-data.json: TRAD_PIECES, COMPANIONS[side][garmentId], PIECE_SIDE, NAM_OF
- presets.json: 35 ready outfits
- misc.json: LEVELS 1–3
- scenes.json: pop-up layers per áo
- compare.json: Đừng nhầm pairs
- copy-giay.json: every sentence on screen
- stage.json: where every standing paper piece of Gặp áo goes on the lying book, the decorations per chapter and
  their collage level, and the paths of the paper pictures in public/book/
